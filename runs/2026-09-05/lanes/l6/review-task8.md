| Critical | `model/viewport.ts` — `transformFor`, the `translateXPx` line | `transformFor`'s translate term divides by the **rendered** viewport's µs-per-pixel (`usPerPixel`, computed from `renderedSpanUs`) instead of the **current** viewport's. This is correct only when `renderedSpanUs === currentSpanUs` (pure pan) or when `current.startUs === rendered.startUs` (which is only true for a zoom anchored exactly at `pixelX = 0`) — i.e. it happens to pass all three of this commit's `transformFor` tests, none of which combine a span change with a non-zero start shift.
