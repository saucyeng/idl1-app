## Verdict rationale

The Rust and TS mechanics of "copy the five trees, verify sha256, rebuild catalog, then switch" are competently built and tested — the missing-root policy (task 1) and the delete-guard scanner (task 3) are solid. But the lane implemented and shipped `move_data_dir` as a **copy**, which is not what the current, ratified spec says: ruling R197 (same day, explicitly "the dataroot lane told") amended C3 §3.10 and C4 §1 to require move-with-delete semantics specifically because disk may not hold two copies of a library. The lane's branches in both repos never merged `main` after R197/R198 landed, and the app-repo diff as it stands would revert those rulings and delete another lane's queued OTA brief if merged. This is not a documentation nit — it is the wrong feature, built to a superseded contract, plus a merge that would corrupt the shared ledger.
