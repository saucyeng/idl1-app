**Test quality.** `table.rs`'s and `verbs/mod.rs`'s tests are the load-bearing ones for R230 and are properly Arrange/Act/Assert, one behaviour per test, and genuinely exercise the generated clap tree (`every_table_row_becomes_a_reachable_subcommand`, `a_verb_outside_the_table_is_a_parse_error`) rather than re-asserting the table's own constants. `session.rs`'s `dash_if_empty` tests and `workbook.rs`'s `kind_name`/`eval_cell_json` tests are small but not vacuous — they pin observable JSON shape. No test exercises the `--track` lap-context path end-to-end (the Critical finding above would have been caught by one).

**R230 compliance.** The clap tree is genuinely generated (`verbs::augment` walks `rows_for(noun)`, no hand-written subcommand duplicates a row) and a real test (`a_verb_outside_the_table_is_a_parse_error`, plus the Rust and TS vocabulary-enumeration tests) would fail on a stray noun/verb. `--json`/`--data-dir`/`--dry-run`/exit codes are uniform by construction (`CommandRow::all_flags`, enforced by `every_row_takes_json_and_every_writer_takes_dry_run` and the clap-level `every_writer_accepts_dry_run_and_no_reader_does`), not just documented. Layering is clean: `core/src/commands/*` has no clap/tauri import and does no CLI-only logic; the `library`/`docs` adapters in `cli/src/verbs/store.rs` correctly rebuild the pre-existing `LibraryAction`/`DocsAction` enums field-for-field (spot-checked against `library.rs`'s enum — no dropped or mis-typed flags found). The `--report`/CI-regeneration omissions (C6 §4.2, §5 item 4) are honestly recorded with sound reasoning (no Rust HTML renderer; CI step belongs to a different lane) rather than silently dropped.

**Verdict rationale.** The command-table architecture, generation, closed-vocabulary enforcement and uniform-behaviour tests are solid and exactly what R230 asked for, and the two acknowledged gaps are properly owned. But the `lap_context()` gap is a genuine silent-wrong-answer bug in exactly the place the task asked to scrutinize: a session with a designated main lap will silently report whole-session numbers under `--track`, with no error and no doc warning, for the CLI's canonical lap-aware use case. That is a blocking correctness defect, not a nit.

VERDICT: NEEDS_FIXES
