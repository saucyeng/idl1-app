## Verdict rationale

The one real problem is the colour-field regression: R212 item 5 explicitly promises "no change to what the form edits," and the stroke-colour control's editable value space silently shrank from "any string" to "8 tokens + clear," which is a genuine, unauthorized behavioural narrowing rather than a pure refactor — it needs either a fix (custom-value entry) or an explicit ruling accepting the narrowing. The two stale doc-comment references to the deleted `commitDrag` are minor but should be swept up. Nothing here rises to a rework-level architectural problem.
