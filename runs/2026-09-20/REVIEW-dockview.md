## Verdict rationale

The layout document shape (`namedDockLayout`, `panelStates`, `activeGroup`) was checked byte-for-byte against
`dockview-core` 8.3.1's real typings and bundled source (`SerializedDockview`, `SerializedGridObject`,
`GroupPanelViewState`, `GroupviewPanelState`, and the `fromJSON`/`BaseGrid.getPanel` resolution of `activeGroup`
as a *group* id, not a panel id) and is correct; `sanitizeDockLayout`/`dockLayoutPanelIds` reject every malformed
input traced (cyclic trees, missing panels entries, duplicate views, wrong version) without throwing. The
epoch/`applyingRef` guards correctly prevent a dock-originated layout from being pushed back through `fromJSON`
mid-gesture, and the settle-timer debounce does avoid a `localStorage` write per drag tick. However, the panel
*presence* round trip between `DockFrame`, `studioColumns.ts` and the Notebook page's own never-all-off guard has
a real, verifiable dead end: closing the dock down to its last panel and then closing that panel too strands the
studio empty with no ribbon/View-menu recovery, directly contradicting brief item 2 and the CHANGELOG's own
description of the feature. That is a correctness bug in the exact "does it do what the brief says, no more no
less" and "loops/never recovers" categories this review was asked to prioritize, so this cannot pass as-is.
